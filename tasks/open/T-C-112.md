## What needs to change

## Why it's needed
An admin or faculty member asking "what is the status of the grading pipeline?" gets a
capability refusal: no tool returns draft vs committed grade counts.

## Proposed change (draft)
- Mutates: false. Requires approval: false. Allowed roles: `faculty` (of the course), `admin`.
- Input: `{ course_id?: string, assignment_id?: string }` (admin may omit `course_id` for all
  courses; the gateway refuses an unbounded scope for everyone else).
- Output: `{ assignments: [{ assignment_node, course_node, title, submissions, drafts,
  committed }] }`.
