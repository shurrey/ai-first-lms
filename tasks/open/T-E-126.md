## Goal
Scope transcript and profile access by policy and by course.

## Context
- Course scoping comes from the Scholar feedback: AI in one course must not expose a student's whole course load.

## Acceptance criteria
Copied from spec.md; the source section is in parentheses after each item.
- [ ] Faculty/advisor transcript access follows `data.instructor_transcript_access`: `summary` (default: analyst summary + concepts, no raw text), `full` (raw, access logged), `none` (§12.6).
- [ ] Course-scoped sessions get a learner profile filtered to that course's observations unless `data.cross_course_profile` is `on`, or the learner opted in under `learner_opt_in` (§12.6).
- [ ] With default policy, Dr. Torres cannot open Emma's raw tutor transcript; with `full`, she can, and it is logged (§12.8).
- [ ] A CS 101 tutor session does not receive Emma's ENG 102 profile observations when `data.cross_course_profile=learner_opt_in` and she has not opted in (§12.8).
