## Goal
Make `requires_approval` tools halt the live turn and resume only on a human approval.

## Context
- `ApprovalGate.create_request` is currently never called (spec.md §2).

## Acceptance criteria
Copied from spec.md; the source section is in parentheses after each item.
- [ ] For `requires_approval: true` tools: do not execute; call `ApprovalGate.create_request(...)`, emit `approval_request`, persist the pending approval; suspend the turn (`awaiting_approval`) and resume on `POST /api/approval` (§5.2 step 5).
- [ ] The approver must have permission for that tool and scope, checked again on resume (§5.2 step 5).
- [ ] Enforced for every §5.3 tool. Approval endpoints are human-only; approvals carry `approved_by = ctx.person_id` (§5.3).
- [ ] Scenario 3 (grading) halts at `approval_request` in the live path, not with mocks. Approving commits; rejecting does not (§5.5).
